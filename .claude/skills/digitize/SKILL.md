# Digitize — turning printed tables into verified data

A workflow for digitizing scanned tables (census volumes, statistical abstracts, archival
records) into audited CSVs, using two waves of subagents with deliberately different jobs:

- **Wave 1 — readers.** Cheap, parallel, one page each. They transcribe what is printed and
  nothing else. **Readers are blind: they are never told the arithmetic identities.** When a
  reader was once given the identities "to check its work", it rewrote whole columns into
  internal consistency — fabricated values that satisfied every check. Identity failures fell
  from 45/72 to 9/72 and the improvement was fiction. Blindness is the architecture's central
  rule, not a style choice.
- **Wave 2 — adjudicators.** Careful, sequential, one unit of work each. They hold the
  identities as a QC instrument, read flagged cells against the print at high resolution, and
  write corrections to an append-only sidecar. A human rules on everything an adjudicator
  cannot settle; lessons accrete forward from unit to unit.

The identities — arithmetic that must hold within a printed column (parts sum to totals,
cross-panel reprints agree) — are the load-bearing wall: wave 2's triage keys on them. A table
with no identities runs in a degraded spot-check mode that `digitize-setup` will flag.

## The phases

| skill | when |
|---|---|
| `digitize-setup` | Once per project. Interviews you about the table, then scaffolds the substrate: `digitization/MANUAL.md`, lessons files, queues, sidecar convention, and the project-specific tool scripts. |
| `digitize-extract` | Wave 1. Fans readers out over the page manifest until every page has a committed extract. Completeness checks only — no quality judgment. |
| `digitize-adjudicate` | Wave 2. Drives the sequential per-unit adjudication loop; dispatches one `digitize-unit` subagent at a time and independently verifies its exit criteria. |

`digitize-unit` is the wave-2 subagent — dispatched by the orchestrator, not invoked by hand.

## Lifecycle

setup → extract until the manifest is exhausted → adjudicate until every unit is closed or
queued → you rule on the queues → re-open passes apply your rulings → repeat until the RULING
queue is empty and every unit is `final`.

All project state lives in the project's `digitization/` directory, generated by setup. The
skills carry the process; the substrate carries everything project-specific.
