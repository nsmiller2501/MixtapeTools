# Grossman 1972 JPE

**Summary**: Grossman models health as a durable capital stock that produces healthy time and can be maintained through costly investment (source: Grossman_1972_JPE.pdf). The paper supplies the health-production foundation for the project's split between anticipatory defensive stock and contemporaneous defensive action (source: Grossman_1972_JPE.pdf).

**Sources**: Grossman_1972_JPE.pdf

**Last updated**: 2026-05-03

## Project-Relevant Takeaway

Grossman's key move is to treat medical care as a derived demand: people buy care, spend time, and use other inputs because they want the underlying commodity "good health" (source: Grossman_1972_JPE.pdf). Health is a durable stock $H_i$ that produces healthy time and depreciates with age (source: Grossman_1972_JPE.pdf). This lets the project treat defensive action against ozone as an input into health production rather than as an outcome in itself (source: Grossman_1972_JPE.pdf).

## Health Capital

The health accumulation equation is:

$$
H_{i+1} - H_i = I_i - \delta_i H_i,
$$

where $I_i$ is gross health investment and $\delta_i$ is depreciation (source: Grossman_1972_JPE.pdf). Gross investment is produced with medical care, own time, and human capital:

$$
I_i = I_i(M_i, TH_i; E_i).
$$

For the project, $k$ can be interpreted as a stock or bundle of ex-ante defensive investments that affects the health-production function before the daily ozone shock arrives (source: Grossman_1972_JPE.pdf; source: Carleton_etal_2024_HESECC.pdf).

## Healthy Time And Fiscal Outcomes

Grossman distinguishes the stock of health from the service flow of healthy time (source: Grossman_1972_JPE.pdf). Sick time is costly because it reduces time available for market and nonmarket activity, and medical care is one input used to maintain health capital (source: Grossman_1972_JPE.pdf).

This distinction matters for the project because Medicare admissions and costs are downstream outcomes of the health-production process, while defensive action changes the mapping from ozone exposure into those outcomes (source: Grossman_1972_JPE.pdf).

## Mapping To The Ozone Project

The project combines Grossman's health-production logic with Carleton et al.'s adaptation timing (source: Grossman_1972_JPE.pdf; source: Carleton_etal_2024_HESECC.pdf). Expected ozone exposure $P_{im}$ can shape ex-ante defensive stock $k$, while daily shocks $\epsilon_{it}$ affect contemporaneous defensive action $b_t$ and residual health outcomes.

The coefficient gap $\beta^S - \beta^N$ should therefore be interpreted as a reduced-form object tied to health production: it measures the difference between health responses to unexpected and expected ozone, not a direct observation of all defensive inputs (source: Grossman_1972_JPE.pdf; source: Carleton_etal_2024_HESECC.pdf).
